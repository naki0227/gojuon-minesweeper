## Description
Minesweeper meets word guessing.

The board is the alphabet (or the Japanese kana chart). The letters of a hidden word are the mines. Open a cell and its number tells you how many answer letters are next to it. Orange cells are letters in the answer. Put the clues together and guess the word!

HOW TO PLAY
• Guess the answer, or pass and open a cell for a clue
• Numbers show how many answer letters touch that cell
• Hit a mine and you lose your next chance to answer
• First to name the word wins

MODES
• Two players on one device
• Online: play a friend with a room code, or get a random match
• Endless: solve word after word and chase a high score
• Daily puzzle: a new one every morning (only once the in-app daily puzzle ships)

FEATURES
• 7,000+ English words and 14,000+ Japanese words, in genres from animals and food to science and geography
• Choose word length and genres
• Short, friendly tutorial
• Sounds and music you can turn off

Free to play with ads. Remove ads with a one-time purchase. Gems for hints and revives are optional.
